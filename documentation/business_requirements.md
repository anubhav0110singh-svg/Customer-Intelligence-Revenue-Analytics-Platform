# Business Requirements

## Project Title

Customer Intelligence & Revenue Analytics Platform

## Business Problem

A telecom company is experiencing customer churn and potential revenue loss. The organization needs an analytics solution to understand customer behavior, identify churn drivers, analyze revenue exposure, and identify high-risk customer segments.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to build an end-to-end analytics solution using Microsoft Fabric to transform raw customer data into actionable business insights.

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the overall customer churn rate?
2. Which customer segments have the highest churn rate?
3. Which contract types are associated with higher churn?
4. Which services contribute the most to customer churn?
5. How much revenue is potentially at risk due to churn?
6. Which customers should be prioritized for retention strategies?

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Customers
- Active Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate
- Total Revenue
- Revenue at Risk
- Average Monthly Charges
- High-Risk Customers

## Expected Outcome

The final solution will provide an end-to-end analytics platform that helps stakeholders understand customer churn, identify revenue risks, and support data-driven customer retention decisions.
